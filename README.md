# Super-Store-Sales-Analysis

Analysis of retail sales data (2015–2018) to identify sales trends, top-performing categories, and regional performance. Includes an interactive dashboard built with Streamlit.

## Dataset
[Superstore Sales Dataset — Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final)

## Questions Explored
- How have sales changed over time?
- Which category generates the most sales?
- Which region generates the most sales?
- What are the top 10 best-selling products?
- How is sales split across customer segments?
- Which shipping mode is used most often?

## Key Findings
- Sales show an overall upward trend from 2015 to 2018, with strong seasonality — sales peak toward the end of each year (Sep–Dec) and dip in January.
- The West and East regions generate the highest sales, while the South region lags behind, suggesting an opportunity for regional expansion.
- The Consumer segment alone accounts for over 50% of total sales, making it the primary driver of revenue.

## Tools Used
Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Streamlit
